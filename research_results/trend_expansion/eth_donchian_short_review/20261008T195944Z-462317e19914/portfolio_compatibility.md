# RMM BTC 4h + ETH SHORT 1h: feasibility only

No combined return, correlation, exposure or portfolio drawdown was calculated.
The compact RMM final-falsification `conclusion.json` is PREPARED_NOT_EXECUTED;
A–D are INCONCLUSIVE/NOT_EXECUTED, demo_approved=false. That document cannot provide
a synchronized return panel. Other older RMM experiments may contain historical
evidence, but none was nominated here as a matching frozen-account portfolio pair.

Local BTC RMM sessions also exist. The session header
`results/forward/rmm_btc_4h/20261008T152507Z-067f7231387d/session.json` records a start
on2026-10-08 and status running at inspection. A header is not proof of valid signals,
completed trades, financial performance or any operational approval. Journals and
live session files were not modified or financially analyzed. ETH historical evidence
ends2026-09-26, so this observed October session does not form a concurrent pair.

Researching the combination is plausible: different asset, cadence and direction.
BTC and ETH share crypto risk; a short sleeve can lose during sharp bull moves while
momentum changes sides. Bearish/high-volatility concentration of ETH entries does
not demonstrate negative correlation or a hedge. Low-frequency controls do not
remove simultaneous gap risk or cost concentration.

Required evidence before an actual portfolio study:

- Nominate immutable RMM candidate/version/run IDs, ledger/equity hashes, dataset
  fingerprints and exact overlapping UTC period with ETH; use all six ETH variants
  without selecting by the resulting portfolio return.
- Shared quote numeraire, capital denominator and explicit sleeve weights frozen
  before looking at results. Align marked equity/cash to1h UTC; treat 4h signals as
  available only at the4h close. Separate terminal liquidations and account resets.
- Audited positions/entries/exits and both cost ledgers, not only summary returns.
  Intrabar ETH exit timestamps are unknown; measure overlap using conservative
  candle intervals and disclose bounds rather than inventing exact times.
- Compute synchronized net return correlation with dependence uncertainty,
  overlapping exposure hours, joint notional/risk, simultaneous drawdowns and
  aggregate fees/slippage/spread under both cost assumptions. Preserve loss gaps.
- Freeze shared capital/exposure limits and collision/rebalance policy. Synthetic
  short collateral, RMM volatility targeting, funding and actual contract margin
  must be reconciled. Two independent10000 accounts cannot simply be represented
  as a single10000 portfolio; summing returns is invalid.
- Review BTC/ETH concentrations and bearish/high-volatility episodes, and evaluate
  on new joint chronological data rather than tuning sleeve weights on these years.

No modification to RMM, its risk, data, runner, configs or sessions is required for
this feasibility note. No diversification benefit or trading approval is asserted.
